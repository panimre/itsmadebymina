Good to know♥

How long do orders take?

Ready-made items ship in 2–4 business days. Personalized and custom pieces take 7–10 business days after you approve your proof.

Do you ship?

Yes, within the U.S. Local pickup in Broward County is free; choose it at checkout or on your custom request.

Can I return a personalized item?

Personalized and custom items are made just for you, so they're final sale. If something arrives damaged or wrong, reach out within 7 days and I'll make it right.

How do I pay?

After I confirm your order or quote, pay by Zelle to or ask for a secure card payment link. Zelle payments can't be canceled, so please wait for my confirmation before sending.

How do digital products work?

After purchase you receive a download link by email. Digital planners work in GoodNotes, Notability and most PDF apps.
